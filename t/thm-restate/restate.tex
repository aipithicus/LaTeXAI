\section{Introduction}

Here is our primary result:
\begin{restatable}[Main Convergence]{theorem}{thmConvergence}
\label{thm:conv}
Let $(x_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence such that $x_n \to x^*$ as $n \to \infty$.
Then for any continuous function $f$, we have:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:limit}
\lim_{n \to \infty} f(x_n) = f(x^*).
\end{equation}
\end{restatable}

An intervening theorem to advance the counter:
\begin{theorem}
Every bounded sequence has a convergent subsequence.
\end{theorem}

A deferred lemma declared with restatable*:
\begin{restatable*}{lemma}{lemBound}
There exists a constant $M > 0$ such that $|f(x)| \le M$ for all $x$.
\end{restatable*}
