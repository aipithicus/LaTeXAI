\begin{algorithm}
\caption{Binary Search}\label{alg:binsearch}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Sorted array $A$, key $k$\label{line:req}
\Ensure Index of $k$ or $-1$\label{line:ens}
\State $l \gets 1$\label{line:initl}
\State $r \gets n$\label{line:initr}
\While{$l \le r$}\label{line:while}
  \State $m \gets \lfloor (l + r) / 2 \rfloor$\label{line:mid}
  \If{$A[m] = k$}\label{line:if}
    \State \Return $m$\label{line:retm}
  \ElsIf{$A[m] < k$}\label{line:elsif}
    \State $l \gets m + 1$\label{line:right}
  \Else\label{line:else}
    \State $r \gets m - 1$\label{line:left}
  \EndIf\label{line:endif}
\EndWhile\label{line:endwhile}
\State \Return $-1$\label{line:retfail}
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

In Algorithm~\ref{alg:binsearch}, line~\ref{line:req} states inputs and line~\ref{line:ens} states outputs.
Initialization occurs at lines~\ref{line:initl} and~\ref{line:initr}.
The loop at line~\ref{line:while} computes the midpoint at line~\ref{line:mid}.
The match condition is checked at line~\ref{line:if}, returning at line~\ref{line:retm}.
Branching continues at lines~\ref{line:elsif}, \ref{line:right}, \ref{line:else}, and \ref{line:left}.
The loop terminates at line~\ref{line:endwhile}, falling through to line~\ref{line:retfail}.
